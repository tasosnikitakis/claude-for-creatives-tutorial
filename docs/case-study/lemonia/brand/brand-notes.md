# Brand notes — what Lemonia already has

*Fictional client. This is the raw material you will turn into DESIGN.md in
Module 5. It is incomplete and slightly inconsistent, like real brand notes.*

## Logo

A hand-drawn lemon branch with the word "Lemonia" in a soft serif, drawn by
a local illustrator in 2019. Used on the paper bags, cups and the shop sign.
Exists only as a PNG (2000px wide) and a photo of the original drawing. No
vector file. Do not redraw or "modernise" it.

## Colours (from the paper bags and sign)

- Bag kraft brown — roughly `#c8a97e`
- Sign green (the shutters) — roughly `#3f5a3c`
- Lemon yellow (the lemon in the logo) — roughly `#e8c547`
- Whitewashed wall — roughly `#f7f3ea`
- Ink on the menu board — near black, `#222222`

The owner says: "The green is the most us. The yellow is only for the lemon,
never big."

## Type

- The menu board is chalk, handwritten.
- The printed menu uses a serif that looks like Georgia (it is Georgia).
- No brand typeface exists. The owner likes "something that looks like an old
  Greek schoolbook, but readable".

## Photography

- Nikos's phone photos: natural light, the courtyard, flour on hands, pies on
  the old marble counter. Slightly underexposed, which they like.
- Never: flat-lay styled shots, people posing with coffee, filters.
- Every product photo needs a plain description for alt text; Nikos will
  supply the names, not descriptions.

## Signage and print

- Shop sign: green on whitewash, logo left, "Lemonia — Αρτοποιείο · Καφέ".
- Paper bags: kraft with the green logo.
- Workshop flyers (2025): A5, photo top, three lines of text, a QR code to
  Instagram.

## Things the owner has said about the look

- "Like my grandmother's kitchen, not like a magazine."
- "People should see the prices without searching."
- "Big enough text for my mother to read on her phone."
