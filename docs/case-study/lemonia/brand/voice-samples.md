# Voice samples — approved by the owner

*Fictional client. Five pieces of writing the owner says "sound like us".
Use these in Module 1 to reverse-engineer the brand-voice skill. Greek
originals are given where they exist; the English versions were written by
the owner, not translated by a tool.*

## 1. Menu board intro (chalk, by the till)

> Everything here was baked this morning, in this house, by Nikos. If
> something has run out, it is because it was good. Ask us what is coming
> out of the oven next.

> Όλα όσα βλέπετε ψήθηκαν σήμερα το πρωί, σε αυτό το σπίτι, από τον Νίκο.
> Αν κάτι τελείωσε, είναι γιατί ήταν καλό. Ρωτήστε μας τι βγαίνει μετά από
> τον φούρνο.

## 2. Instagram caption (winter, leek pie)

> Leek pie is back. Same pastry as the spanakopita, more pepper, and the
> leeks come from Mr Stavros at the Saturday market. Until the leeks run out.

## 3. Pre-order reply (WhatsApp, to a customer)

> Hello Maria, yes, a lemon pie for 8 people on Saturday is fine. It will be
> ready from 10:00. It is €26 and you pay when you collect. If anything
> changes, just reply here. Eleni

## 4. Sign on the door (August)

> We are closed from 15 to 31 August, because bakers need the sea too.
> Back on Tuesday 1 September, with fresh bread from 7.

> Κλειστά 15–31 Αυγούστου, γιατί και οι φούρναρηδες θέλουν θάλασσα.
> Επιστρέφουμε την Τρίτη 1 Σεπτεμβρίου, με φρέσκο ψωμί από τις 7.

## 5. Workshop description (flyer, 2025)

> Saturday sourdough, 10:00–13:00. Six people, one long table, flour
> everywhere. You leave with a loaf, a jar of our starter, and the
> confidence to do it again at home. €45, coffee and pie included.

## What the owner says about these

- "We talk like we talk at the counter. Short. Friendly. No selling."
- "We say exactly what it costs and when it is ready."
- "A little joke is fine. Two is too many."
- "We never say sorry for running out. Running out is a good sign."
- In Greek we use **εσείς** on signs and the website, **εσύ** only in
  WhatsApp with people we know.
