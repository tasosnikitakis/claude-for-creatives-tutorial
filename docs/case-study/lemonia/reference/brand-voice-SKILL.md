---
name: brand-voice
description: Apply Lemonia Bakehouse's voice to any website copy, microcopy, sign, caption or message, in Greek and English.
---
Audience: neighbours who come daily, families ordering for occasions, summer
visitors on their phones. Many read Greek first; some only English.

Voice: like talking at the counter. Short, friendly, plain. Says exactly what
it costs and when it is ready. Never sells.

Tone by context:
- Menu and product: what it is, what is in it, the price. One detail that
  shows it was made here ("leeks from the Saturday market").
- Pre-order confirmation: the item, the day, the time it is ready, the price,
  "you pay when you collect". Sign off with Eleni's name in messages.
- Sold out: state it plainly. Never apologise; running out is a good sign.
- Closed: say when and when you are back, with one light reason.
- Errors on the site: calm, say what to do next, offer the phone number.
- Workshops: the time, the number of people, what you leave with, the price.

Humour: at most one light line per piece. Never in errors or allergen notes.

Terminology:
- Say "baked this morning", not "freshly baked" or "artisan".
- Say "pre-order", not "book" or "reserve". Greek: "προπαραγγελία".
- Say "collect", not "pick up". Greek: "παραλαβή".
- Say "ask for a place" for workshops; Eleni confirms. Greek: "ζητήστε θέση".
- Never: artisanal, curated, elevated, passion, indulge, treat yourself.

Hard rules:
- Prices always shown, with €. Times in 24-hour format (10:00).
- Sentence case. No exclamation marks anywhere.
- Allergen notes use the exact kitchen line: "Everything is made in one
  kitchen that also uses nuts and sesame. We cannot promise any item is free
  from traces." Never shorten it.
- Greek: εσείς on the website and signs. Use content/glossary-el.md for every
  term. Mark new Greek lines "awaiting native review".

Examples (approved):
1. "Everything here was baked this morning, in this house, by Nikos. If something has run out, it is because it was good."
2. "Hello Maria, yes, a lemon pie for 8 people on Saturday is fine. It will be ready from 10:00. It is €26 and you pay when you collect."
3. "We are closed from 15 to 31 August, because bakers need the sea too."

Before returning copy: check every rule above and the element's length
limit in content/microcopy-matrix.md. Offer three options with a one-line
rationale unless asked for one.
