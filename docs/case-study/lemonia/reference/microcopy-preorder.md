## Notes for review

- The success line assumes a form service that actually delivers the order. If the client chooses the pre-filled email link instead, nothing is received until the customer presses send in their own email, so the line becomes: "Your email app will open with the pre-order ready. Press send, and Eleni will confirm within a day."
- The button uses "Στείλτε την προπαραγγελία" rather than the glossary's "Κάντε προπαραγγελία", because at this point the order is already filled in and the action is sending it. Log the decision in glossary-el.md if the native reviewer agrees.
- "Within a day" is a promise: confirm with the owner that she can keep it.
- The button says the action and the success line repeats it ("Send pre-order" → "Pre-order received").
- The allergen helper uses the kitchen's exact wording, unshortened.
- No exclamation marks, no apologies, phone number offered on failure.
