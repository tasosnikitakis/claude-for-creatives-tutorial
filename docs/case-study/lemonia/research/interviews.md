# Customer conversations — notes

*Fictional research. Six short conversations held at the counter and on the
phone in March. Names are invented. Notes are close to verbatim where in
quotes; the rest is the interviewer's summary. Use in Module 1 for the
synthesis exercise.*

---

## C1 — local, 60s, comes daily

- Buys bread and a tyropita every morning.
- Ordered a name-day cake for her husband; "you called me the day before to
  check, that's why I trust you".
- Does not use the website. "Why would I? I'm here every day."
- Daughter in London orders cakes for her by phone. "She had to call three
  times because nobody answered at lunchtime."

## C2 — local, 40s, two children

- Orders whole pies for children's parties, about once a month.
- "I order on Instagram but I never know if you've seen the message."
- Would like to see "what I can order and how many days before".
- Allergies: her son has a nut allergy. "I always have to ask if the
  koulourakia have almonds. They don't, but I ask every time."

## C3 — summer visitor, 30s, from the Netherlands

- Found Lemonia on maps; came three times in one week.
- "The pies had no labels in English. I pointed and hoped."
- Wanted to know opening hours on Sunday; the website said 15:00, she came at
  16:00 and it was open. "So I didn't trust the website."
- Would book a workshop "if I had known before my last day".

## C4 — local, 30s, works from home nearby

- Comes in the afternoon to work with a coffee.
- "I don't care about the website. I care that the Wi-Fi works."
- Heard about workshops from a friend; "I would have booked if I'd seen it
  anywhere".

## C5 — gift buyer, 50s, lives across town

- Bought a workshop place as a birthday gift for his wife.
- "I had to message you on Instagram, wait a day, then come in to pay."
- Wanted "a page I can send her, so she knows what it is".
- Would pay online if it were possible; "but cash at the door is fine too".

## C6 — local, 70s, the owner's former teacher

- Uses a phone "only for calls and photos of my grandchildren".
- "The writing on websites is always too small."
- Pre-orders tsoureki every Easter by phone. "I will keep calling. But my
  son would use a website."

---

## Interviewer's notes

- Four of six mentioned pre-ordering. Three mentioned not knowing if an
  order or message was received.
- Two mentioned the wrong opening hours.
- Two mentioned workshops they would have booked had they known.
- One mentioned allergies; she asks every time.
- Nobody mentioned a blog.
