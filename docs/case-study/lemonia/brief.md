# Brief — Lemonia Bakehouse website

*Fictional client. Written by the owner, Eleni, in her own words, then
tidied up after a first call. Some of it is vague on purpose: a real brief
always is.*

## About us

Lemonia is a small bakehouse and café near the sea in a quiet Athens suburb.
We opened in 2019 in my grandmother's old house; the lemon tree in the
courtyard gave us the name. We bake everything on site from 6am: sourdough,
koulourakia, lemon pie, seasonal tarts, and savoury pies (spanakopita,
tyropita, a leek pie in winter). Coffee is from a small roaster in Piraeus.
Twelve seats inside, twenty in the courtyard.

Team: me, my brother Nikos (head baker), and three part-time staff.

## The problem

- Our website is a single page someone made in 2019. It says we close at 15:00
  (we close at 18:00 now) and it has no menu.
- Most people find us on maps or Instagram. Visitors in summer cannot read
  the Greek-only signs and ask the same questions all day.
- Cake pre-orders come in by phone, Instagram messages and WhatsApp. We miss
  some. We have twice baked a name-day cake for the wrong day.
- We started Saturday baking workshops (sourdough, koulourakia) last spring
  and they sell out, but people only hear about them by word of mouth.

## What we want

1. A website in Greek and English that feels like the place: warm, simple,
   a bit old-fashioned, not "Instagram café".
2. The menu, with prices and allergens, easy to update ourselves.
3. A clear way to pre-order whole cakes and pies at least 48 hours ahead,
   which sends us an email we can't miss. (No online payment for now — they
   pay when they collect.)
4. A page for the workshops, with dates and a way to ask for a place.
5. Opening hours that are right, and say clearly that we close on Mondays
   and for two weeks in late August.

## Who it is for

- Locals, mostly 30–70, who already love us and want to order a cake.
- Summer visitors staying nearby, often on their phones, often in English.
- People who want a gift or a workshop for someone else.

## Things we never want

- Stock photos. We will provide our own photos (Nikos takes them on his phone).
- Words like "artisanal", "curated", "elevated", "passion".
- Anything that makes us look expensive. A slice of pie is €3.80.
- Pop-ups asking for an email address.
