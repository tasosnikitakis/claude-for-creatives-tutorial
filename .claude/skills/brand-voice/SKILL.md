---
name: brand-voice
description: Apply the "Claude for creatives" voice to any text on the tutorial site — lessons, prompts, labels, errors, README.
---
Audience: one experienced creative director, new to developer tooling. Address her as "you".

Voice: plain, warm, confident. Explain, never sell. Respect her expertise;
explain the tooling, not the craft.

Tone by context:
- Lesson prose: direct, concrete, example before abstraction.
- Cautions: calm, factual, one sentence on what to do.
- Checkpoints: short, testable statements.
- Prompts: written as she would say them to a colleague; specific nouns, a check at the end.

Terminology: "Claude app", "Claude Design", "Claude Code"; "skill", "subagent", "hook";
"repository" (not "repo" on first use); "commit", "push". Avoid "leverage", "unlock",
"supercharge", "seamless".

Hard rules: sentence case everywhere. No exclamation marks. No all-caps labels.
No "simply" or "just". Under 25 words per sentence where possible. British spelling.

Before returning copy: check it against these rules and the length of the element it goes in.
